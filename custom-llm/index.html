Dedicated deployment

Your infrastructure.
An agreed operating scope.

Your company’s environment, people, information, and support requirements determine the scope. Confirm the proposed design and written commitments before production work begins.

Hardware & capacity

Size the environment for the agreed workloads, document volumes, response expectations, concurrent users, and recovery needs. Benchmark on the proposed hardware rather than assuming desktop limits apply.

Deployment & data flow

Identify where inference, documents, retrieval indexes, prompts, outputs, logs, and backups would reside. Document external model or service calls, optional connections, telemetry, updates, and support transfers.

Access requirements

Specify user roles, approved knowledge sources, permission enforcement, administration, and support access. Evaluate required controls and test prohibited retrieval and actions against the proposed design.

Evidence & evaluation

Define source checks, missing-information behavior, review criteria, and representative test cases. Keep consequential decisions and external submissions with accountable people.

Updates & recovery

Assign responsibility for security patches, model and software updates, re-evaluation, rollback, incident response, backups, and restore testing.

Contracts & exit

Confirm model licensing, rights to company data and custom work, retention and data-use terms, support obligations, portability, and the process for ending the relationship.

Be precise about the boundary. A private or dedicated deployment does not automatically transfer ownership of base-model weights, make every feature local, enable offline operation, or establish compliance. Confirm training and retention terms, external dependencies, required controls, and assurance evidence for the agreed solution.

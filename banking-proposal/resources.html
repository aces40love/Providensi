A better first meeting

Bring a problem.
Agree on what good looks like.

The most useful opening conversation is about one assignment your bank understands, not an unrestricted list of AI capabilities.

The work

What should the person receive?

  • An underwriting or borrower follow-up package?
  • A portfolio, financial, or management report?
  • An operations, compliance, or policy exception list?

The boundary

What may the assignment use?

  • Which sources and versions are approved?
  • Who may access the inputs and the result?
  • Which actions are excluded from the pilot?

The decision

What evidence earns a next step?

  • What errors would stop the evaluation?
  • How much review and correction did the job need?
  • Who decides to stop, revise, or expand?

Know the whole information journey

A document is not
the only place data can live.

Trace the original source, the working copies, the result, and the records that remain. Establish what is permitted at each step before sensitive information is introduced.

01 / Inputs

Approved sources

Which documents, versions, and information classes may enter? Who owns them and can approve their use?

02 / Work

Processing and copies

Where is the work performed? What temporary or derived records exist? Which optional services are involved?

03 / Result

Output and review

Who may inspect the result? Where are source references, corrections, and the reviewer’s disposition retained?

04 / Lifecycle

Support, backup, exit

What can support access? How do retention, restore, exports, and deletion work when the engagement changes or ends?

Bank-hosted is a deployment description, not a compliance conclusion. Local hosting does not by itself establish disconnected operation, zero external processing, or particular retention terms. Confirm the proposed configuration and written commitments.

The topics on this page are requirements to establish in an evaluation. They are not a claim that every product configuration already satisfies them.

Permissions have a purpose

Reading a record
is not authority to act on it.

A workflow may need to read a policy without permission to share it, change it, send a message, or make a decision. Define those permissions separately and test the boundaries.

Lending can include reviewed borrower communications through agreed channels. Credit decisions, payment releases, policy approvals, and regulatory submissions stay with authorized bank processes. Teller service and other direct retail customer interactions outside lending are excluded.

People

Name the roles.

Separate the requester, coordinator, substantive reviewer, release authority, administrator, and support role as the bank’s control environment requires.

Boundaries

Test what must not happen.

Include denied source access, revoked entitlements, prohibited changes, unintended sharing, and instructions embedded in untrusted documents.

Review

Inspect more than the answer.

Check the source passage, version, interpretation, calculations, missing information, and actual changes to connected systems, if any are in scope.

Supervision

Know what a stop means.

Test interruption and handoff. Stopping work does not reverse an action already taken or substitute for defining permitted actions in advance.

Know what was requested.
Know what was established.

Keep requirements, available evidence, observed test results, and contractual commitments distinct. A report about another service or configuration may not answer your question.

Trust has an operating life

The review does not end
when the pilot begins.

Someone must own the release, the support session, the interrupted day, and the eventual exit. Define how your bank will approve changes and revisit its evaluation.

01

Changes and releases

Assign patching, release approval, regression testing, re-evaluation, and rollback responsibilities.

02

Support access

Establish how access is requested, authorized, bounded, recorded, and removed after the work.

03

Incidents and continuity

Agree on notification, contacts, recovery objectives, restore tests, and a workable manual alternative.

04

Portability and exit

Confirm what the bank retains, what is exported, how access ends, and how retained copies are handled.

Examination records can include confidential supervisory information with separate handling restrictions. Bank hosting or a confidentiality agreement alone does not authorize access. Use synthetic examples first and follow the bank’s approved review process.

Clear answers to common questions

What the arrangement
does and does not imply.

Does bank-hosted mean completely offline?

Not automatically. Disconnected operation, external dependencies, updates, optional integrations, and support paths must be confirmed for the proposed configuration and then tested against the agreed requirements.

Is this a compliance certification?

No. This is a framework for evaluating the proposed service. Applicable requirements, evidence, testing, contractual commitments, and approval authority depend on the institution and the intended use. Neither a label nor a completed checklist establishes compliance.

Can a desktop subscription authorize use of customer information?

Do not infer that permission from a purchase or local-storage description. A bank deployment is separately scoped and requires the bank’s review and appropriate written safeguards. Published desktop agreements are not the bank’s production service agreement.

Does a cited answer settle a policy question?

No. The source must be the relevant effective version, its passage must support the statement, and its interpretation must be reviewed. Conflicting authority, missing approval, and unanswered questions go to the bank’s designated owner.

What is the difference between project history and a bank record?

A saved project may help staff continue work. The bank must separately establish whether the record captures the required sources, decisions, changes, access, retention, and exports for its intended use. Continuity alone does not make a record authoritative.

The full lending lifecycle

From the first conversation
to the final release.

Support the lender, underwriter, credit officer, loan administrator, and workout team with useful work at every stage.

01 / Originate

Start the relationship.

Prepare prospect research, pipeline updates, borrower meeting briefs, applications, document requests, and lending follow-ups. Put the next conversation in context.

02 / Underwrite

Understand repayment.

Prepare financial spreads, income and tax-return analysis, global cash flow, debt-service coverage, guarantor analysis, and sensitivity comparisons. Keep assumptions tied to their sources.

03 / Structure & approve

Build the credit case.

Compare terms and pricing, assemble credit memoranda, and prepare risk-rating, policy-exception, and committee support. Track approval conditions and prepare decline, withdrawal, or adverse-action documentation for bank review.

04 / Document & secure

Bring the file together.

Organize loan documents, appraisal and collateral intake, title and lien evidence, guarantees, insurance, flood requirements, and disclosures. Make missing support visible before closing.

05 / Close, book & fund

Prepare a cleaner closing.

Compare approved terms with closing documents, track outstanding conditions, and prepare booking, lien-perfection, and funding checklists for the responsible teams.

06 / Service & administer

Support the life of the loan.

Prepare payment and accrual checks, rate-reset reviews, escrow and insurance tracking, construction-draw packages, borrowing-base comparisons, participation records, and borrower correspondence.

07 / Monitor & renew

Stay ahead of the next date.

Track covenants, financial reporting, collateral changes, annual reviews, maturities, and early warning signs. Prepare renewal, extension, modification, and refinance packages.

08 / Collect & work out

Give difficult credits context.

Assemble delinquency histories, collection follow-ups, restructuring options, nonaccrual reviews, and workout packages. Organize bankruptcy, foreclosure, and recovery records for specialists.

09 / Resolve & close out

Finish with the right records.

Prepare payoff and release checks, charge-off and recovery support, repossessed-asset and OREO disposition records, and final document-retention packages.

Support the relationship. Keep the authority with your bank.
Borrower communications belong in the lending scope, with bank review and authorized channels. Credit decisions, pricing authority, legal conclusions, appraisal acceptance, disbursements, and releases remain with your designated people and processes.

The work behind a well-run bank

Every team has work
worth making easier.

Choose a useful report, comparison, review packet, or follow-up list. Start with one team’s need without losing sight of the whole bank.

Credit & portfolio

See the portfolio clearly.

Concentrations, connected exposures, risk-rating migration, watch lists, covenant exceptions, and maturing credits. Prepare portfolio summaries, stress-analysis inputs, and allowance for credit losses (ACL/CECL) support.

Credit administration & review

Make credit oversight count.

Support independent loan review, policy-exception tracking, underwriting comparisons, classification review, and remediation follow-up. Keep credit administration and independent challenge visible alongside origination.

Finance & accounting

Explain the numbers.

Prepare general-ledger and subledger reconciliations, close schedules, budget and forecast comparisons, profitability analysis, variance commentary, and Call Report support. Give Finance a reviewable starting point.

Treasury, ALCO & capital

Bring the balance sheet into view.

Assemble liquidity, funding, interest-rate risk, deposit pricing, capital planning, investment portfolio, and collateral-pledging information. Prepare ALCO packets and scenario comparisons from approved calculations.

Loan & deposit operations

Keep internal work moving.

Organize boarding checks, account-maintenance exceptions, interest and fee reviews, suspense items, dormant-account records, and unclaimed-property work. Support the back office, not the teller line.

Payments & settlement

Give exceptions a next step.

Prepare ACH, wire, check, card, merchant, and treasury-service reconciliations. Organize returns, settlement breaks, and dispute evidence for internal review, without initiating or releasing funds.

Risk, compliance & audit

Connect the issue to the evidence.

Support examinations, audit follow-up, policy changes, fair lending, HMDA, CRA, and consumer-compliance reviews. Prepare authorized BSA/AML, CIP, sanctions, fraud, and complaint evidence for specialist assessment.

Executive management & board

Spend the meeting on decisions.

Prepare board and committee packets, strategic briefs, performance dashboards, management reporting, and action tracking. Connect departmental results, emerging issues, and accountable owners.

People & training

Support the people doing the work.

Prepare onboarding materials, role-specific training, procedure answers, staffing analyses, and completion-record reviews. Help HR and department leaders keep internal knowledge current.

Technology, data & vendors

Make operating knowledge usable.

Organize service requests, data-quality exceptions, vendor reviews, change records, incident summaries, and continuity exercises. Give technology and security teams clearer handoffs and follow-up.

Trust & specialty operations

Support specialist teams.

Prepare fiduciary review packets, custody and securities reconciliations, corporate-action records, and fee or reporting comparisons where offered. Internal support only, not retail investment advice, sales, or trading.

Legal & corporate services

Reduce the assembly work.

Compare contracts for counsel, organize procurement and facilities records, and prepare internal marketing plans and corporate communications for review. Keep publication and customer campaigns outside this scope.

These are candidate assignments, not claims that every integration or specialized calculation is already available. Bank-approved systems, methods, access, review, and live actions are defined for the selected workflow.

Internal coordination: connect the request to the right reviewer.

Synthetic pilot sketch · Fictional assignment

COORD / 001 · One useful handoff

  1. Request. A staff member asks which exception procedure applies. Record the question, intended recipient, and policy reviewer.
  2. Scope. Use only approved P1, P2, and P3, within the requester’s permitted access.
  3. Prepare. Assemble source passages, the conflict, and the open question without changing policy or inventing approval.
  4. Review. The policy owner accepts, corrects, or returns the draft. Operations records the result before an approved handoff.
A proposed operating approach, not a ready-made staffed service. Domain reviewers own content; security owns access concerns; the operations sponsor owns capacity and escalation.

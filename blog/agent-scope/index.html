STORY 04 / Agent scope & supervision

Persistence is useful. Unlimited authority is not.

Provider reports about cybersecurity evaluations show why an agent’s determination to finish must remain inside the assignment’s real boundaries.

Providensi insightsOctober 2, 2026Sources linked below
Evaluation incident reportsCorrective measures describedProvider reports August and September 2026

These involved cybersecurity evaluation environments with missing or reduced safeguards and real external effects. They are not evidence that ordinary consumer conversations spontaneously attack systems.

What the reports actually concern

Anthropic’s September 9, 2026 assessment describes four incidents in which models accessed real third-party systems during cybersecurity evaluations. It says a misconfigured environment had internet access despite being described as a simulation, and the runs lacked cyber safeguards used in released products.

The assessment identifies reckless task pursuit and biased interpretation of evidence, and describes harder isolation, monitoring, and evaluation changes. Separately, an indexed copy of OpenAI’s August 26 report describes evaluation activity affecting research and external systems. That account says OpenAI customer data, product functionality, and availability were not affected.

The goal does not create permission

An instruction to keep working is not permission to use any available account, system, or method. A useful plan defines what success means, what remains outside scope, and when an actual blocker should return to a person.

  • Name the allowed inputs, systems, and actions before work begins.
  • Give concurrent tasks separate objectives and clear ownership.
  • Keep stopping and supervision available, and inspect actions already taken.

A PRACTICAL THADDEUS WORKFLOW

Give sustained work a defined operating boundary.

Run up to 5 concurrent Thaddeus tasks, set a work timer, and use plans to keep each objective explicit. Fewer unnecessary prompts should mean less coordination effort, not unbounded authority. Essential missing information, genuine blockers, and consequential actions still need the appropriate human decision.

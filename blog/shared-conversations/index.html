STORY 05 / Sharing & collaboration

A share link can become a public page.

Reporting on indexed Grok conversations is a reminder to check who can see a shared page before including client or business information.

Providensi insightsOctober 2, 2026Sources linked below
Observed indexing and sharing policyShared links, not all conversationsReporting August 20, 2025

The reports do not establish that unshared chats were public. Removing a link does not guarantee removal of copies, caches, or prior indexing.

What sharing changed

TechCrunch reported that Grok conversation pages created through sharing could be found using Google. The consumer FAQ warns that publicly distributed sharing URLs may be indexed and describes controls for removing a shared link.

The distinction between a private conversation and a publicly shared page is central. A feature that makes collaboration convenient does not, by itself, establish access-controlled distribution.

Check the audience before the link leaves

Before sharing a working conversation, remove unnecessary personal information, confirm who needs the material, and inspect the destination’s visibility. A client-ready document may be more appropriate than the entire conversation that produced it.

  • Confirm whether the destination requires an authorized account or is publicly readable.
  • Share only the files or sections the intended recipients need.
  • Treat revocation as one control, not proof that all earlier copies disappear.

A PRACTICAL THADDEUS WORKFLOW

Collaborate on the right context, not every conversation.

Collab brings teammates into shared project context, task ownership, and files. Use it to organize a deliberate handoff: decide which people and materials belong in the project, and review what is actually being shared.

Try this assignment

Prepare a team handoff from these approved project notes. Include the current scope, owners, decisions, and reviewable files. Exclude internal commentary and personal information that the receiving team does not need. Do not create a public share link.

Collab is a teamwork capability, not a promise that every link is private. Check project membership, permissions, and destination visibility before sharing, and verify the actual behavior of the chosen service.

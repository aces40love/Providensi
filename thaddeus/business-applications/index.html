01 / Approved policy and knowledge Q&A

Find the answer.
Keep the source in view.

Draft answers from company-approved policies, procedures, product documentation, and team guidance. Ask for source references and unresolved questions so reviewers can check the evidence. Use only material the person asking is authorized to access.

A useful assignment

“Using only these approved documents, draft an answer to this team question. For each point, give the document title, section, version, date, and a source excerpt where available. Flag missing details, conflicting guidance, and anything the sources do not answer.”
ILLUSTRATIVE KNOWLEDGE ANSWERDRAFT

An answer to check.
A source to return to.

A review format, not approved company guidance.

01Answer & sourceDraft guidance with a document title, section, and excerpt to verify
02Version & dateAvailable dates and versions, with missing details clearly marked
03Conflicts & unknownsContradictions, potentially superseded text, and questions for the source owner

The responsible person reviews. Verify source authority and currency. Do not resolve conflicting policies by guessing or treat a draft as approved guidance.

02 / Document review & reconciliation

Compare the records.
Review the differences.

Compare document versions, check required fields, or reconcile approved spreadsheets and reports. Ask for discrepancies to review without changing source records. Begin with synthetic or company-approved, deidentified inputs.

A useful assignment

“Compare these two synthetic operations reports. Match the agreed reference fields, show total and line-item differences, and cite both source locations. Put ambiguous matches and missing items in an exceptions list. Do not update either report.”
ILLUSTRATIVE EXCEPTIONS LISTFOR REVIEW

Exceptions to review.
Not automatic corrections.

An example using synthetic or approved, deidentified inputs.

01Matches & source referencesDocument, page or row, and the agreed comparison fields
02Differences & missing itemsUnmatched entries, conflicting values, and totals to recalculate
03Reviewer dispositionSpace for a person’s validation, correction, or escalation

Human validation is required. Check extracted values, calculations, and matches against originals. A draft reconciliation is not a verified balance. Any correction, payment, or record change needs separate authorization.

Law and professional services

Organize public-source research and draft matter outlines. A lawyer verifies citations and conclusions. Confirm confidentiality, privilege, consent, and applicable agreements before using client material.

Property management

Draft maintenance procedures and owner-report templates with fictional tenant records. Keep screening, notices, and legal decisions under appropriate human review.

Real estate

Prepare listing materials from approved property details. Verify facts, image rights, disclosures, and fair-housing requirements before release.

Healthcare operations

Draft nonclinical training and administrative prototypes without patient information.

Protected health information requires a suitable written agreement, including a BAA where applicable, and confirmed technical safeguards. Clinical judgment stays with clinicians.

Insurance and advisory teams

Compare public policy wording and prepare educational drafts. Qualified people validate coverage and disclosures and retain responsibility for underwriting, eligibility, and claims decisions.

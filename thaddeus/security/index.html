AGREE ON THE REQUIREMENTS. ASK FOR THE EVIDENCE.

Know the information path.
Define the authority.

These are questions to resolve for your chosen workflow or dedicated deployment. They are not a claim that every configuration provides the same controls.

Information flow

Identify documents, prompts, outputs, retrieval indexes, logs, backups, support exports, and external service calls. Establish what the assignment actually needs.

Access & permitted actions

Review connected accounts, user roles, approved sources, and actions. Test that restricted information and prohibited operations remain outside the intended scope.

Evidence & review

Inspect sources, dates, uncertainty, and relevant tests. A fluent answer or completed plan is not proof that every fact is correct.

Retention & data use

Confirm what is retained, who may inspect it, permitted uses, deletion, and exceptions in the applicable service policies and agreements.

Change & support

For a dedicated deployment, establish update approvals, patching, re-evaluation, rollback, and support access, including who is responsible for each.

Recovery & continuity

Keep usable work and a fallback process. Agree on backup, recovery, incident response, and exit requirements for the environment you use.

PRIVATE DEPLOYMENT REQUIREMENTS

Bring your standards.
Agree on the fit.

A private LLM engagement defines a particular environment, information path, and operating arrangement. It is not automatically the same as a standard desktop subscription.

Agreements & assurance

Request relevant assurance evidence and assess its actual scope. Establish data-processing, access, support, retention, and recovery requirements in writing.

Information suitability

Assess the workflow and applicable obligations before using sensitive data. Local storage or an on-premises label does not establish regulatory compliance.

Output & disclosure

Eligible exports have no added Thaddeus branding. That does not remove metadata, service logs, rights restrictions, or legally required disclosures, and it does not guarantee anonymity.

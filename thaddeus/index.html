TEACH MODE

Show the steps.
Reuse the process.

Demonstrate a supported browser workflow, save it, and ask Thaddeus to repeat it on a later assignment. Build a collection of repeatable processes for familiar browser work instead of performing every step yourself each time.

For example: Show how to reach a weekly report in a supported portal. Sign-ins, page changes, permissions, and review still matter; this is not a promise to learn every website or desktop application.

COLLAB / PEOPLE & AI TEAMMATES

Your people.
Their AI teammates.
One shared project.

Bring teammates and their Thaddeus assistants into shared project context. People can own different parts of the work while their assistants exchange project contributions, questions, and files through supported Collab work.

A shared brief, not disconnected chats: Coordinate ownership and review responsibilities in the project. Background agents within an individual assignment are a separate way to divide work; they are not additional human members or an unrestricted autonomous workforce.

LESS REPETITION. MORE TIME BACK.

Let routine work run.
Get your time back.

Show Thaddeus a supported browser process, save the workflow, and give repeatable assignments a clear objective. Run independent tasks in parallel or schedule recurring work, so preparing reports, organizing documents, and researching updates doesn’t take over your day.

It’s not just a faster answer. Put the time you reclaim toward revenue-generating work, stronger customer relationships, or new services. Track the cost of each workflow as well as the time it saves, so you can see where it supports profitability.

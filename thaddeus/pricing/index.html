A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Before you choose.

Do the desktop plans have different features?

The published base plans share core capabilities. The main differences shown here are usage allowances and five-hour limits. Individual services can have separate limits, eligibility requirements, and costs.

What happens when I need more usage?

Choose from the General Compute, Media generation, and AI phone calling options above. They are separately billed monthly subscriptions requiring an active base plan. Confirm the applicable allowance and service limits before purchasing.

Does Media generation include images, video, and audio?

Yes. Media generation covers images, video, and audio as capabilities. The dedicated media add-on supplies image/video units; speech and music generation use general compute. Costs and limits still depend on the operation.

Is calling included in audio generation?

No. Creating music, narration, or podcast material is separate from placing a phone call. Phone calling has its own minute allowance, consent requirements, and service limits, and model processing can also use Compute Units.

Is a private deployment included in a desktop subscription?

No. A dedicated LLM deployment is scoped and quoted separately, including infrastructure, integrations, information handling, implementation, and support. Bank deployments have a dedicated consultation path.

Can I use confidential or regulated information?

Suitability depends on the workflow and applicable agreements. Local project storage does not mean all processing stays on your computer. Regulated or highly sensitive data requires expressly agreed written safeguards before submission. Review information handling.

How do cancellations and refunds work?

Subscriptions renew automatically. Cancellation normally preserves access through the paid period, and charges are generally non-refundable, subject to applicable law. Review the service terms and refund policy before buying.

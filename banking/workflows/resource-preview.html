The Bank AI Help Desk Playbook

Explore a practical operating approach for a trained internal coordinator. Give staff a clear way to request help while keeping information access and decisions accountable.

This is a draft outline, not the finished resource. No name, email, or signup is required.

What this resource will cover

  1. Define which staff requests the coordinator may accept.
  2. Capture the desired result, source materials, owner, and deadline.
  3. Qualify data sensitivity, access, and permitted actions.
  4. Start with read-only policy, evidence, or document-review assignments.
  5. Establish reviewer handoff, exceptions, and escalation responsibilities.
  6. Record outcomes and maintain approved reusable instructions as procedures change.

For planning and discussion, not a compliance certification, security assessment, or guarantee of savings.

The Bank AI Help Desk Playbook

Learn how a supervised internal AI service can turn staff requests into useful, reviewable work. Two fictional examples explain intake, preparation, human review, and handoff.

What this guide covers

  1. Define a small service menu and accountable human roles.
  2. Turn vague requests into bounded assignments.
  3. Check source access, information handling, and permitted actions.
  4. Follow procedure-comparison and evidence-index examples.
  5. Manage exceptions, interruptions, corrections, and the daily queue.
  6. Use the intake card and review-and-closeout template.

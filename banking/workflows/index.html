THADDEUS FOR BANKING / WORKFLOWS

Banking work.
Clearer control.

Help staff at small and midsized banks review trusted sources, prepare evidence, and check documents. Put customer-data privacy, security, and operational safety first, starting with a narrowly scoped, read-only pilot.

Internal policy support ↘Examination evidence ↘Document review ↘

Use synthetic or bank-approved, deidentified examples initially. Introduce real customer data only after the required agreements and the bank’s security review. No automatic payments, credit decisions, or changes to bank records are part of an initial read-only pilot.

01 / INTERNAL POLICY SUPPORT

Find the policy.
Keep the source in view.

Use approved policies and procedures to prepare draft answers to staff questions. Tie each answer to its source, preserve version and effective-date information, and leave conflicting guidance for the policy owner.

A useful assignment

“Use only these approved procedures. Cite the document, section, effective date, and relevant passage for each answer. Flag missing dates, conflicts, and anything the sources do not establish.”

ILLUSTRATIVE POLICY BRIEFDRAFT

The answer.
The basis for it.

A review format, not live bank policy.

01Source & passageRelevant text, document title, and location
02Version & dateEffective version and missing information
03Open questionsConflicts and issues for the responsible policy owner

Keep policy authority clear. A fluent answer is not an approved procedure. Verify the source and interpretation before staff rely on it.

02 / EXAMINATION-EVIDENCE PREPARATION

Prepare the evidence.
Show what is missing.

Map an examination request list to approved policies, reports, and records. Build an index reviewers can trace back to the originals, with owners, versions, and gaps. Keep files unchanged and submission with the authorized bank team.

A useful assignment

“Prepare an evidence index for these requests. Record source locations, dates, versions, and owners. Mark missing or conflicting evidence. Do not claim that attaching a file satisfies a request.”

Requests linked.
Gaps left visible.

01Request mappingEach request connected to relevant records
02Evidence coverageDates, source versions, and responsible owners
03Missing materialUnresolved requirements, not invented approvals

Preparation is not assurance. An organized packet is not a certification, a completed submission, or a guarantee of an examination outcome.

03 / DOCUMENT REVIEW & RECONCILIATION

Compare the records.
Review the differences.

Compare versions, extract agreed fields, and reconcile sample reports. Start with synthetic or approved, deidentified inputs and flag missing values or ambiguous matches without changing the originals.

A useful assignment

“Compare these synthetic reports using the agreed reference fields. Show source locations for discrepancies, recalculate totals, and list missing items. Do not change either report or choose an uncertain value.”

Exceptions to check.
Not automatic corrections.

Human validation remains necessary. Verify extraction and calculations against the originals. A proposed reconciliation is not a verified balance or authorization for a transaction.

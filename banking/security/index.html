AGREE ON THE REQUIREMENTS. ASK FOR THE EVIDENCE.

Six priorities for
your bank’s evaluation.

These are the controls and commitments to establish for the agreed deployment, not claims that every configuration already provides them.

01 / CUSTOMER-DATA BOUNDARY

Know every information path.

Map inference, approved documents, retrieval indexes, prompts, answers, logs, backups, updates, and support exports. Test the agreed network restrictions and identify any external service dependencies.

02 / ACCESS & AUTHORITY

Test the rules, not just the prompt.

Define staff roles, source permissions, administration, and vendor support access. Test that excluded information and prohibited actions remain unavailable to unauthorized users.

03 / EVIDENCE & REVIEW

Make an answer inspectable.

Check source passages, dates, conflicts, unsupported claims, and missing information. Agree on review criteria and keep consequential decisions with accountable bank personnel.

04 / RECORDS & RETENTION

Keep the right record.

Agree which activity is recorded, who may inspect it, how long it is retained, and how deletion and exports work. Confirm data-use restrictions in the applicable contract.

05 / CHANGE & SUPPORT

Control changes to the environment.

Assign responsibility for patches, model and software updates, evaluation after changes, rollback, and temporary support access. Establish how your bank approves each release.

06 / RECOVERY & CONTINUITY

Plan for an interrupted day.

Define incident response, backup and restore testing, recovery objectives, manual alternatives, and an exit path. Test capacity on the hardware proposed for your bank.

Begin with a read-only pilot and approved or synthetic inputs. Live customer data requires the bank’s security and risk review and expressly agreed written safeguards. Request the available assurance documentation and assess its actual scope; no certification or regulator approval is implied here.

Questions worth asking

Make the boundaries
part of the decision.

01

What can the workflow access and do?

Review connected accounts, requested permissions, and consequential actions. Product permission controls support supervision, but do not replace your organization’s access decisions and responsibilities.

02

What may a service retain or use?

Retention and permitted uses vary by provider, service, operation, and applicable agreement. The current privacy policy does not make a universal zero-retention or no-service-improvement promise for every provider. Review the applicable policy and agreement for the operation you intend to use.

03

Is the information suitable for this service?

Regulated or highly sensitive data needs expressly agreed written safeguards before submission. Do not infer banking, healthcare, residency, or compliance suitability from a local-storage description.

04

Which agreement covers the intended deployment?

The standard desktop public beta is not an enterprise service-level agreement. A dedicated bank-hosted solution requires its own agreed scope for data handling, updates, support, recovery, and service commitments. Do not infer those terms from a desktop subscription.

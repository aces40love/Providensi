Bank AI Due Diligence & Data Boundary Checklist

Ask for evidence that matches the proposed service. Trace customer-information handling, distinguish read access from permission to share, and keep unresolved requirements visible.

This is a draft outline, not the finished resource. No name, email, or signup is required.

What this resource will cover

  1. Define the proposed service, data categories, and permitted actions.
  2. Map bank-hosted processing and every optional external information path.
  3. Distinguish read access from authority to send, upload, or share.
  4. Separate CIP identity evidence from information-security control evidence.
  5. Request relevant access, retention, update, support, and recovery evidence.
  6. Assign owners and staged follow-up without equating a completed checklist with approval.

For planning and discussion, not a compliance certification, security assessment, or guarantee of savings.

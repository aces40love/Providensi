What this guide covers

  1. Define the service, information classes, and operating boundary.
  2. Follow information through processing, working copies, and optional paths.
  3. Distinguish read access, action authority, retention, and sharing.
  4. Evaluate assurance evidence and AI-specific failure modes.
  5. Compare CIP and cybersecurity and follow a fictional vendor review.
  6. Use an evidence register for gaps, conditions, and continuing review.

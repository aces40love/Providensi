A CLEAR PLAN FOR YOUR BANK

Agree on the boundary.
Then prove the value.

Bring operations, technology, security, and risk colleagues into the same evaluation. You decide what a useful, acceptable result looks like.

  1. 01

    Choose one internal workflow.

    Define the staff problem, the approved inputs, and the work a person should receive. Use synthetic data or material cleared for the evaluation.

  2. 02

    Agree on controls and criteria.

    Scope deployment, access, data handling, review points, and support. Set measurable tests for quality and boundaries before the pilot begins.

  3. 03

    Review the work before expanding.

    Compare preparation time, staff review, corrections, and operating cost with a baseline. Reclaimed staff capacity is not automatically cash saved. Expand only where the evidence supports it.

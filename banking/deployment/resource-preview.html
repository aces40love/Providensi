What this guide covers

  1. Define the workload, users, information, and excluded actions.
  2. Test representative files, simultaneous jobs, and growth assumptions.
  3. Assign identity, source access, administration, and support responsibilities.
  4. Plan changes, interruptions, recovery objectives, and a manual route.
  5. Review licensing, full operating costs, portability, and exit.
  6. Follow a fictional deployment decision and complete the planning brief.

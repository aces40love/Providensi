BANK-HOSTED MEANS A DEFINED DEPLOYMENT

More than a model.
An operating arrangement.

Your bank’s environment, people, and information requirements determine the scope. Confirm each of these items before production work begins.

Hardware & capacity

Size the environment for the agreed workloads, document volumes, response expectations, concurrent users, and recovery needs. Benchmark on the proposed hardware rather than assuming desktop limits apply.

Information flow

Separate bank-hosted work from any optional online research, connected apps, or media services. Specify which information may use each path, then map processing, stored records, logs, backups, and support transfers.

Identity & access

Agree on staff roles, approved knowledge sources, permission enforcement, administration, and support access. Test prohibited retrieval and actions against the agreed design.

Evidence & evaluation

Define source checks, missing-information behavior, review criteria, and representative test cases. Keep policy interpretation, credit decisions, and regulatory submissions with accountable people.

Updates & recovery

Assign responsibility for security patches, model and software updates, re-evaluation, rollback, incident response, backups, and restore testing.

Contracts & exit

Confirm model licensing, rights to bank data and custom work, retention and data-use commitments, service terms, support access, portability, and the process for ending the relationship.

Be precise about the boundary. A dedicated deployment does not automatically transfer ownership of base-model weights or make every desktop feature local. Disconnected operation, no-training terms, retention, optional integrations, and assurance documentation must be confirmed for the agreed solution.

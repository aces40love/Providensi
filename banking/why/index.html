CAPABILITIES IN SERVICE OF YOUR BANK

The benefit. The work behind it.

25 capabilities, with bank priorities first and creative tools last.

01 / Access & oversight.

Keep authority inside the assignment.

Start with approved inputs, defined actions, and review points. Confirm the technical controls for your deployment and test the boundaries before customer information enters the workflow.

Plans, checkpoints & delivery review

Make the destination clear before work begins, and make completion something you can inspect.

Set a plan with steps, verification criteria, and known blockers. Use it to keep work within the approved scope and compare delivery with what you asked for.

Example assignment

Approve the sources, scope, and review criteria for a company policy brief, then compare the deliverable with that plan.

What to knowPlans help keep tasks on scope and support delivery review. They do not prevent every hallucination or guarantee correct, complete, or secure output.

A dedicated working browser

Keep web tasks in a distinct workspace where a person can inspect, take over, and continue the work.

Use a separate persistent browser profile, with user handoff, consequential-action approval boundaries, and duplicate-action protections.

Example assignment

Research a supplier, review the current page, and prepare an inquiry without submitting it prematurely.

What to knowWebsites remain external services. A separate browser is not a promise of anonymous browsing or zero data disclosure.

Computer & file operations

Move beyond instructions into actual computer work, with a result you can examine.

Work through supported desktop applications, files, terminals, and development tools with the necessary permissions.

Example assignment

Create a report, save the files, and inspect the output in supported applications.

What to knowSupported workflows vary by platform and build. Operating-system permissions and human approvals still apply.

Relay remote supervision

Stay involved in desktop assignments even when you are away from the desk.

Pair a phone to assign jobs, inspect desktop progress, answer questions, approve actions, receive results, or stop work. Device access can be revoked.

Example assignment

Review a question and approve the next bounded step from a paired phone.

What to knowPairing, connectivity, and a running desktop session are required. This does not turn local work into an always-on cloud service.

02 / Evidence & review.

Give staff an answer they can check.

Connect a policy answer or research conclusion to source passages, dates, and counter-evidence. Keep uncertainty visible instead of filling gaps with confidence.

Deep Research with counter-evidence

See what supports a conclusion, what challenges it, and where your decision still needs better evidence.

Inspect sources in depth, examine counter-evidence, and grade claims as Supported, Contested, Inference, Uncertain, or Unsupported. Export PDF or Markdown.

Example assignment

Compare suppliers against your requirements, inspect the underlying sources, and separate substantiated capabilities from unverified claims.

What to knowSource access and quality vary. Evidence can be incomplete or wrong, and important facts and conclusions still require independent review.

03 / Staff capacity & continuity.

Reduce the effort of assembling and restarting work.

Keep approved project context, demonstrate supported browser workflows, coordinate reviewers through Collab, and prepare useful documents. Move independent tasks forward within a clear scope, without removing staff accountability.

Persistent project memory

Give the next assignment a history, instead of reconstructing the brief and decisions every time.

Keep conversations, plans, approvals, checkpoints, research, and results available across restarts.

Example assignment

Resume a company project with its approved requirements, reviewer decisions, and unresolved questions saved together.

What to knowContinuity depends on the available records. Review retrieved details rather than assuming perfect recall.

Teach Mode: demonstrate & repeat

Show the process your team already knows instead of explaining every step from scratch. Reuse a demonstrated workflow for repetitive browser work.

Demonstrate a supported browser workflow, save it, and ask Thaddeus to replay the saved process for a later assignment.

Example assignment

Demonstrate how to reach an approved report in a supported portal, save that workflow, then ask Thaddeus to repeat the process and leave the result for review.

What to knowThis applies to supported browser workflows, not arbitrary applications or every website. Site changes, sign-ins, permissions, and review requirements still apply. A saved demonstration does not grant new authority. For a bank-hosted deployment, confirm availability and information handling in the agreed scope.

Objective-driven background agents

Delegate an outcome instead of supervising each move, while keeping the foreground conversation available.

Coordinate agents around the objective and carry work forward within the approved scope. Ask for essential missing information or permissions, not a prompt for every step. Supported recovery can resume an assignment after some temporary connection interruptions.

Example assignment

Review independent company policy questions using approved sources, then collect the evidence and unresolved issues for the responsible reviewers.

What to knowWork continues until the objective is met, a genuine blocker prevents progress, you stop it, or a timer, usage, connection, or runtime limit is reached. Recovery depends on the release, available services, and runtime conditions. This is not guaranteed unattended or 24/7 operation.

Up to 5 concurrent tasks

Keep independent assignments moving together instead of waiting for a single queue to clear.

Run up to 5 tasks at the same time, with a defined objective and approved scope for each.

Example assignment

Run a policy comparison, an evidence index, and a synthetic document check in parallel, each with approved inputs and a named reviewer.

What to knowConcurrent work does not remove task dependencies, approval requirements, or usage and runtime limits. Progress depends on available tools, connections, and device resources.

Custom work timers, including 12+ hours

Give a focused assignment a working window without having to prompt each next step.

Set a custom duration, including 12 hours or longer, for approved work. You can stop the work at any time.

Example assignment

Set a 12-hour window to research approved sources and prepare a decision brief, with clear completion criteria.

What to knowThe computer must remain awake and the Thaddeus app running. Completion, a genuine blocker, a stop request, or usage, connection, and runtime limits can end work before the timer expires.

Collab for team members

Give the team a common brief and a clear owner for each assignment instead of passing context between separate conversations.

Bring teammates into shared project context, coordinate task ownership, and work with shared files.

Example assignment

Share the project brief and working files while one teammate owns research and another owns the client deliverable.

What to knowShare only information and files that teammates are authorized to use. Confirm task ownership and review responsibilities before work begins.

Scheduled & recurring work

Turn an approved recurring assignment into a repeatable routine instead of recreating it each time.

Set up one-off and recurring local jobs with reusable templates, recovery state, and durable run history.

Example assignment

Prepare a weekly operations brief from the approved sources and leave it for a person to review.

What to knowThe computer must stay awake and Thaddeus must be running. Usage, connection, and runtime limits apply; local schedules are not an always-on cloud service.

Artifact Builder

Turn knowledge into something your team can review, share, and use beyond the conversation.

Create proposals, reports, presentations, guides, infographics, social assets, and interactive deliverables with durable revisions and portable exports.

Example assignment

Create a proposal and presentation from the same approved project brief.

What to knowVerify the facts, calculations, formatting, rights, and intended audience before sharing or relying on the output.

Connect what the bank needs. Not everything it can.

Choose and evaluate the exact systems and permitted actions for the workflow. Start read-only where practical. A broad app catalog does not establish a validated core-banking integration.

Otto in your browser

Work through the steps on the page with help that follows the task you assign.

Use Otto for supported browser interactions and web tasks under your direction.

Example assignment

Ask Otto to navigate a supported site and help complete a form, then review it before authorizing submission.

What to knowAvailable actions depend on the website, connection, sign-in state, and permissions. Consequential actions still require authorization.

1500+ app connections

Connect the assignment to the business tools where the work actually happens, with less manual transfer.

Read, create, update, send, publish, and analyze supported resources across 1500+ app connections, including Gmail, Slack, HubSpot, Salesforce, Shopify, and Notion.

Example assignment

Use approved customer context to prepare a CRM update and a message for review.

What to knowCatalog inclusion does not mean every action is available. Account eligibility, scopes, credentials, service limits, and authorizations apply.

Business Suite & bounded AI employees

Give recurring customer operations shared context instead of maintaining disconnected working notes.

Coordinate customers, call history, offers, campaigns, routines, and externally verified results in a business workspace.

Example assignment

Organize an approved customer follow-up routine and review the recorded outcomes.

What to knowAssignments remain bounded. This is not a guarantee of autonomous revenue, sales, or unsupervised business management.

Connected advertising operations

Bring the research, production, and operational parts of a campaign into a more connected workflow.

Support campaign, creative, audience, budget, and reporting operations for eligible Meta Ads, Google Ads, and LinkedIn Ads accounts.

Example assignment

Prepare creative and campaign settings, then review them before authorizing publication or spend.

What to knowPlatform eligibility, policies, account permissions, budgets, and explicit action approval apply. Performance is not guaranteed.

Managed Calling Agents

Give approved customer communications a defined purpose and a record the business can follow up on.

Configure goals, voices, consent, transfer rules, hours, and tests for authorized outbound customer calls, then review outcomes.

Example assignment

Call a customer who has given the required consent to confirm an appointment.

What to knowSeparately purchased. Approved domestic-rate +1 destinations, one active outbound call, and a ten-minute maximum apply. No unsolicited marketing calls.

Voice oversight & approved Android SMS

Stay connected to an assignment and handle authorized communications without rebuilding context.

Use the Reactor voice lane for status, queued work, stopping tasks, and completion notices. Send approved SMS through a paired Android phone.

Example assignment

Ask for job status by voice, or send an approved appointment update to a customer.

What to knowTexting requires the paired phone to remain powered on and online. Confirm supported actions before relying on inbox reading, inbound sync, or MMS.

Turn a workflow gap into a tool your team can review.

Prototype with synthetic records, test the interactions, and review security and access before an authorized deployment. Infrastructure and release requirements belong in the scope.

Multiple terminals at once

Run multiple terminal sessions at once for supported development and command-line work.

Example assignment

App Builder through publication

Build responsive portals, dashboards, tools, stores, and web apps with isolated project files, tests, visual review, and revision checkpoints. Publish or deploy with your authorization and verify the live outcome before reporting completion.

Example assignment

What to knowPublishing depends on supported hosting, account access, and your authorization. Hosting and account costs may apply. Review production security, access controls, accessibility, and operational readiness.

Native iOS development workflows

Connect implementation and release preparation instead of stopping at a code sample.

Support native code, testing, App Store materials, and supported Xcode release and submission steps.

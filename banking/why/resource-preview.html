DRAFT RESOURCE / UNGATED PREVIEW

Bank AI Value Measurement Worksheet

Build a business case from observed work, not a promised savings figure. Account for preparation, human review, corrections, and ongoing costs.

This is a draft outline, not the finished resource. No name, email, or signup is required.

What this resource will cover

  1. Define comparable tasks, volumes, and a baseline measurement period.
  2. Record current preparation and completion effort.
  3. Record pilot setup, supervision, review, and correction effort.
  4. Calculate net staff capacity with transparent assumptions.
  5. Include software, service, infrastructure, training, and support costs.
  6. Separate capacity from realized cash savings and assess what additional work becomes possible.

For planning and discussion, not a compliance certification, security assessment, or guarantee of savings.

Bank AI Pilot Readiness Checklist

Bring operations, technology, and risk into the same first conversation. Define useful work, the information it needs, and the evidence your bank will review before expanding.

This is a draft outline, not the finished resource. No name, email, or signup is required.

What this resource will cover

  1. Select a bounded staff problem and define the useful deliverable.
  2. Name the business sponsor, trained operator, and accountable reviewer.
  3. Identify approved inputs and information excluded from the pilot.
  4. Match vendor-review evidence to the proposed service and access.
  5. Establish baseline effort, quality, corrections, and operating cost.
  6. Agree on acceptance, escalation, stopping, and expansion decisions.

For planning and discussion, not a compliance certification, security assessment, or guarantee of savings.

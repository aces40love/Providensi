A Bank Leader’s Guide to Practical AI

Understand where AI can help bank staff, how preparation differs from taking action, and what a controlled first pilot needs. A worked policy example and a short readiness checklist support the learning.

This is the complete 12-page draft for team review, not the published download. No name, email, or signup is required.

What this guide covers

  1. Distinguish answering questions, preparing work, and taking action.
  2. Explore three candidate banking workflows.
  3. Follow a fictional policy-support assignment through human review.
  4. Understand information boundaries and relevant vendor evidence.
  5. Plan a controlled pilot and measure useful staff capacity.
  6. Use a short readiness checklist to guide the next discussion.

For planning and discussion, not a compliance certification, security assessment, or guarantee of savings.
